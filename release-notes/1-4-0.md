# YayFood 1.4.0 Release Notes

> 1.4.0 was a TestFlight-only release. Everything below reached the App Store as part of [2.0.0](2-0-0.md).

## What's New

### Share Your Gallery
Show off your favorite dishes straight from the Gallery tab:

- **Share Gallery**: A new share button in the Gallery exports a beautiful photo-grid card of the dishes currently in view — share your top-rated dishes, your family's most recent finds, or any filtered view
- **Follows your filters**: Whatever sort and filters you have active is exactly what gets shared

### More Control Over Shared Cards
Card exports are now more flexible:

- **Hide scores**: A new toggle lets you share what you ate without revealing your ratings — layouts adapt cleanly when scores are off
- **Dish notes included**: When "Include personal notes" is on for a restaurant card, each dish's own note now appears too, not just the restaurant note

### Backdate Your Reviews
Logging yesterday's dinner (or last month's trip)?

- **Pick the date**: Adding a dish or a rating now includes a date field, defaulting to today — tap it to record when you actually ate the meal
- **Everywhere it counts**: The date carries through sorting, analytics, exports, and household sync

### Better First Steps
- **Richer getting-started tips**: The welcome screen now introduces the Rating Wizard, Gallery, Map with Discover Nearby, Analytics, and sharing/export features — so new users find the good stuff sooner

### Edit Dishes Faster
You can now make quick changes to a dish without opening the full edit form:

- **Inline name editing**: Tap to rename a dish directly from its detail view
- **Inline photo quick-edit**: Swap or set a dish's photo right from Dish Details -- no need to open the full Edit Dish screen

### Bulk Photo Upload
Adding several dishes at once is now much faster:

- **Multi-photo import**: Select multiple photos at once to create placeholder dishes for a restaurant in a single step
- **Rate later**: The new dishes appear as "unrated" placeholders until you add ratings, so you can capture everything quickly and fill in the details afterward

### Move Dishes Between Restaurants
Logged a dish under the wrong place? You can now fix it without re-entering anything:

- **Move to Restaurant**: Reassign a dish to a different restaurant from the Dish Details menu using a searchable restaurant picker
- **Everything comes along**: The dish's ratings, notes, and photo move with it automatically

### Smarter Search
Finding dishes is more flexible:

- **Search by location**: The Dishes tab search now matches a restaurant's address and notes, not just names -- so you can find that dish even when you only remember where you had it

### Better "Sort by Rating"
Sorting restaurants by rating now reflects the bigger picture:

- **Depth counts**: Restaurants with more rated dishes are favored, so a place with several great dishes ranks above one riding on a single high rating

## Improvements & Fixes

- **Live counts when filtering**: The All Dishes view's restaurant and dish counts now update as you search or filter
- **Cleaner tab switching**: Search text clears automatically when you switch between the Restaurants and Dishes tabs
- **Unfiltered move targets**: The "Move to Restaurant" picker now lists every restaurant, regardless of any filter active in your lists

## Requirements

- **iOS**: 17.0 or later
- **Storage**: Minimal -- all photos stored locally with aggressive compression
- **Internet**: Only needed for optional Pexels image fetching -- everything else works offline
