# YayFood 2.0.0 Release Notes

YayFood's biggest release yet: your food diary is now something you can share with the people you eat with. Everything you already love still works exactly the same, fully offline and instant — household sharing is entirely optional and layered on top.

## What's New

### Household Sharing (optional)
Keep one shared food diary with your family or roommates:

- **Create or join a household**: Sign in with Apple, create a household, and invite others with a private, expiring invite link
- **One shared pool**: Restaurants, dishes, photos, and everyone's ratings sync across all members' devices automatically
- **Everyone's taste, tracked separately**: See who rated what — each person's ratings stay attributed to them, even when someone logs a rating on another's behalf
- **Shared dish photos**: Photos you add sync to the whole household, downloaded on demand to keep things fast and light
- **Unobtrusive sync status**: A subtle indicator shows when the household is syncing, offline, or fully up to date — with a Sync Details view if anything needs attention
- **Pick up where you left off**: Sign in on a new iPhone and your household is restored automatically
- **Leave and keep your copy**: If you leave a household, your device keeps a private copy of everything — nothing is lost. (The person who created a household hands it over to another member before leaving.)

### Sign in with Apple & Account Control
- **Private by design**: Household sharing uses Sign in with Apple — no passwords to create or remember
- **Delete your account in-app**: A complete account-deletion flow lives in Settings › Household — it removes your identity from our servers while leaving the household's shared history intact for the others (and your device keeps a personal copy)

### Rating Check-Up
Break false ties in your ratings with quick, blind comparisons:

- **Find your real favorites**: When several dishes share the same score, Rating Check-Up shows them two at a time — no numbers, just the food — and nudges each by ±0.1 based on your picks
- **Three scopes**: Run it app-wide (Settings › Analytics), for a single restaurant, or for one dish against its exact-score ties
- **Nothing changes until you say so**: Every proposed adjustment is listed for review with per-row toggles; nothing is saved until you tap Apply

### Accessible Rating Entry
- **Type or tap**: Enter a rating by typing the exact number, nudging it ±0.1 with fine-tune buttons, or using the slider — all three stay in sync

### Share Your Gallery
- **Share Gallery**: A new share button in the Gallery exports a photo-grid card of the dishes currently in view — your top-rated dishes, your family's most recent finds, or any filtered view
- **Follows your filters**: Whatever sort and filters you have active is exactly what gets shared

### More Control Over Shared Cards
- **Hide scores**: A new toggle lets you share what you ate without revealing your ratings — layouts adapt cleanly when scores are off
- **Dish notes included**: When "Include personal notes" is on for a restaurant card, each dish's own note now appears too, not just the restaurant note

### Backdate Your Reviews
- **Pick the date**: Adding a dish or a rating now includes a date field, defaulting to today — tap it to record when you actually ate the meal
- **Everywhere it counts**: The date carries through sorting, analytics, exports, and household sync

### Faster Ways to Add and Fix Dishes
- **Bulk photo upload**: Select multiple photos at once to create placeholder dishes for a restaurant in a single step, then rate them whenever you're ready
- **Move to Restaurant**: Logged a dish under the wrong place? Reassign it from the Dish Details menu — its ratings, notes, and photo move with it
- **Inline editing**: Rename a dish or swap its photo directly from its detail view, without opening the full edit form
- **Sort dishes within a restaurant**: Order a restaurant's dishes by date added, name, or rating

### Smarter Search and Sorting
- **Search by location**: The Dishes tab search now matches a restaurant's address and notes, not just names
- **Better "Sort by Rating"**: Restaurants with more rated dishes are favored, so a place with several great dishes ranks above one riding on a single high rating

### Better First Steps
- **Richer getting-started tips**: The welcome screen now introduces the Rating Wizard, Gallery, Map with Discover Nearby, Analytics, and sharing features — so new users find the good stuff sooner

## Improvements & Fixes

- **Faster dish detail and sharing**: Photo-heavy screens and the Share/Save card exports are noticeably quicker and lighter on memory, even with large libraries
- **Live counts when filtering**: The All Dishes view's restaurant and dish counts now update as you search or filter
- **Cleaner tab switching**: Search text clears automatically when you switch between the Restaurants and Dishes tabs
- **Security hardening**: Sign-in sessions are stored in the device Keychain, and the backend has been hardened across the board ahead of this release

## Privacy

Household sharing is the first YayFood feature that stores data on our servers, so the [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/earlduque/YayFood-Issues/blob/main/Privacy%20Policy.md) and the App Store privacy details have been updated to match:

- **Nothing changes unless you opt in**: If you never join a household, your diary stays on your device and in your private iCloud, exactly as before
- **What a household shares**: The restaurants, dishes, ratings, notes, and photos in the household pool, plus the name you choose to share, are stored on our backend so your household members can see them
- **Still no ads, analytics, or tracking**

## Requirements

- **iOS**: 18.0 or later
- **Storage**: Minimal — photos are aggressively compressed
- **Internet**: Only needed for optional household sync and optional Pexels image fetching — the core diary works fully offline
- **Account**: Only required if you choose to use household sharing; the app is fully usable signed out
